Mirissa Beach
Whale Watching Paradise

Discover the enchanting Mirissa Beach with Grand Ceylon Holidays, a crescent-shaped stretch of golden sand on Sri Lanka's southern coast. Known for its relaxed atmosphere and stunning natural beauty, Mirissa offers the perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. This picturesque beach destination has become famous worldwide for its incredible whale and dolphin watching opportunities, with blue whales, sperm whales, and playful dolphins frequently spotted in these waters.

Grand Ceylon Holidays invites you to experience Mirissa's perfect blend of tranquility and adventure. Start your day with a sunrise whale watching excursion, spend the afternoon relaxing under swaying palm trees, and end your day with a spectacular sunset view from Parrot Rock. The beach is lined with charming restaurants and cafes serving fresh seafood and tropical drinks, creating the perfect atmosphere for unforgettable evenings by the ocean. With its pristine waters and laid-back vibe, Mirissa offers an authentic Sri Lankan beach experience that will leave you with lasting memories.

Key Highlights

  • World-class whale and dolphin watching experiences

  • Pristine crescent-shaped beach with calm swimming areas

  • Parrot Rock viewpoint for spectacular sunset panoramas

  • Excellent surfing spots for beginners and intermediate surfers

  • Beachfront dining with fresh seafood and tropical cocktails
