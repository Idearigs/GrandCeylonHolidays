Wildlife & Adventure
Minneriya National Park
Home of The Gathering

Witness one of Asia's most spectacular wildlife events at Minneriya National Park with Grand Ceylon Holidays. This remarkable sanctuary is famous for "The Gathering," the largest known meeting of Asian elephants in the world. During the dry season (July to October), hundreds of elephants migrate to the shores of the ancient Minneriya Tank (reservoir) as water sources elsewhere dry up. This natural phenomenon offers an extraordinary opportunity to observe these magnificent creatures in large numbers as they drink, bathe, and socialize against the backdrop of stunning Sri Lankan landscapes.

Grand Ceylon Holidays provides expertly guided safari tours through Minneriya's diverse habitats, where you'll not only witness the incredible elephant gathering but also spot a variety of other wildlife. The park is home to endemic monkeys, sloth bears, leopards, and over 160 bird species. Our experienced guides know the best locations and times to maximize your wildlife sightings while sharing fascinating insights about elephant behavior and conservation efforts. The ancient Minneriya Tank itself, built in the 3rd century AD by King Mahasena, adds a historical dimension to your wildlife adventure, making Minneriya a perfect blend of natural wonder and cultural heritage.

Key Highlights

  • "The Gathering" - world's largest meeting of Asian elephants (July to October)

  • Ancient Minneriya Tank built in the 3rd century AD

  • Diverse wildlife including endemic monkeys, leopards, and numerous bird species

  • Stunning landscapes of grasslands, scrublands, and tank-side vegetation

  • Excellent photography opportunities with elephants against scenic backdrops
