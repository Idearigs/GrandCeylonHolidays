Paddle Through Paradise: Kayaking Adventures

Discover Sri Lanka's stunning waterways with our expert-led kayaking experiences. Choose from our carefully selected locations, each offering unique landscapes and wildlife encounters.

Top Kayaking Locations
  • Madu Ganga Estuary (Southwest Coast): Navigate through a maze of 64 islands in this UNESCO-listed biosphere, home to rich mangrove ecosystems and abundant birdlife.
  • Bentota River (South Coast): Paddle through calm waters surrounded by tropical greenery, with opportunities to spot water monitors and exotic birds.
  • Koggala Lake (Near Galle): Explore the island-dotted lake, visiting a traditional cinnamon plantation and the famous "Cinnamon Island."
  • Nilwala River (Matara): A quieter alternative with lush riverbanks and excellent birdwatching opportunities.
What to Expect

Our kayaking adventures are suitable for all skill levels, with stable sit-on-top kayaks perfect for beginners. Each tour includes:

  • Safety briefing and basic paddling instruction
  • Guided exploration of hidden waterways
  • Wildlife spotting with expert commentary
  • Stops at points of interest along the route
  • Refreshments and time to swim in safe areas

Our tours are designed with sustainability in mind, following responsible tourism practices to protect the delicate ecosystems we explore. Maximum group size is limited to ensure a personalized experience.
