Discover the World of Ceylon Tea

Immerse yourself in the captivating world of Sri Lanka's tea country with Grand Ceylon Holidays' Tea Trails Experience. Journey through rolling hills covered in emerald-green tea bushes, where the cool mountain air carries the fresh aroma of tea leaves. This full-day exploration takes you to the heart of Sri Lanka's tea-growing regions, where you'll witness the meticulous process that creates the world-renowned Ceylon tea.

Your adventure begins in the misty highlands of Nuwara Eliya, known as 'Little England' for its colonial charm and cool climate. Visit a working tea estate where you'll see expert tea pluckers skillfully harvest the 'two leaves and a bud' that make the finest teas. Learn about the different tea grades and processing methods from experienced planters who have been perfecting their craft for generations.

Continue to a traditional tea factory where the magic happens. Watch as the freshly picked leaves are withered, rolled, fermented, and dried to create the distinct flavors of Ceylon tea. Participate in a guided tea tasting session where you'll learn to distinguish between different tea varieties, from delicate white teas to robust black teas. The experience includes a visit to a tea museum showcasing antique tea-processing equipment and the history of Sri Lanka's tea industry.

Enjoy a delicious lunch at a colonial-era tea planter's bungalow, where you can savor local specialties while taking in panoramic views of the surrounding tea plantations. The day concludes with a visit to a local tea boutique where you can purchase your favorite blends to take home. Grand Ceylon Holidays ensures a comfortable and informative experience with expert local guides who bring the fascinating story of Ceylon tea to life.
